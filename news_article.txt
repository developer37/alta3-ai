Good morning, everyone. In Q3, QuantumLeap Technologies achieved a landmark revenue of $2.5 billion, a 15% increase year-over-year, which speaks to the incredible dedication of our team and the successful integration of our recent acquisitions. We are executing a strategic realignment to better position ourselves for future growth, which includes a workforce optimization initiative that will, regrettably, impact approximately 8% of our global staff but is projected to yield significant operational efficiencies by Q2 of next year.

Our legacy hardware division saw a planned contraction of 5%, as we continue to pivot towards our high-margin AI-as-a-Service platform. This new platform has seen an impressive 200% growth in user adoption, although monetization is still in its early stages. We have also successfully restructured our debt, securing a new credit facility that provides us with operational flexibility, albeit at a higher interest rate in the current macroeconomic climate. We are confident that these bold investments in our future will deliver long-term shareholder value, despite some short-term pressures on our gross margin, which has compressed by 200 basis points this quarter.
